Column d1 · z(t)

One vertical column, z(t). Two columns under the same uplift and the same equilibrium-line altitude: A is weak rock, the landscape's relief reservoir (dR/dt = U − k·Rn); B carries a resistant body that rides up with uplift in the material frame (hypothesis B), and both feel an elevation-dependent glacial rate peaking at the ELA (hypothesis A). Hover any ⓘ or panel title for the equations. Quantities the column cannot resolve are marked asserted.
