Along-valley profile d2_along · z(s, t)

A long profile from divide to outlet under uplift, stream power with Hack's-law drainage area, and a steady-discharge glacier: mass balance routed down the valley gives the ice flux, the SIA flux law inverted by marching upstream gives the thickness, and erosion follows sliding speed. Drainage area and valley width are asserted; ice flux, thickness, sliding and erosion are computed. Hover any ⓘ or panel title for the equations.
