geomorph sandbox illustrating geomorphic hypotheses in 1, 2 and 3 dimensions

The same process physics implemented as parallel tiers: a column, a cross-valley profile, an along-valley profile, a DEM. Each tier computes what it can resolve and asserts the rest, and says which is which. First target: the origin of low-angle uplands in glaciated ranges — planation under deep ice versus uplifted lowland convexities. Hover any ⓘ for the equations. Source and methods: github.com/hig314/geomorph_sandbox.

d1 · Column z(t)

Two lockstep columns under one uplift and ELA history: a weak landscape and a resistant body in the material frame, with an elevation-dependent glacial rate. Relief reservoir kept as a scenario.

d2 · Along-valley profile z(s, t)

Stream power with Hack's-law area; flowline glacier by steady discharge and thickness inversion; sliding-based erosion. In progress.

d2 · Cross-valley profile z(x, t)

U-shape carving, creep, threshold slopes, method-of-slices landslides, plateau-margin retreat. Planned.

d3 · DEM z(x, y, t)

The full coupling: routed steady-discharge glaciers, per-cell strength, scoop landslides. Planned; the legacy DEM model is the starting point.
Static bundle, no build step. Every numerical scheme is validated in Python (py/) before and after porting.
