2D Terrain Evolution Sandbox Methods & references →

A gridded landscape-evolution sandbox (parallel to the 1D uplift model). Seeded initial topography + hillslope creep (linear diffusion or the nonlinear Roering law) + fluvial incision (stream power with D8 flow routing) + uplift. Glacial and deep-failure processes come next. Compute is float32; the map is a hillshade over a hypsometric colormap. See the methods page for equations and references.
