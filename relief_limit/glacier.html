Glacier buttressing R(t)

A stand-alone slide beside Limits to relief: relief and its threshold over the last two glacial cycles, with the ice thickness below. The ice raises the threshold, most sensitively where it is thin and hardly more as it thickens (buttressing, saturating); each change in buttressing fractures the rock and steps the threshold down in small, regular steps (weakening); glacial erosion raises relief at a constant rate. Two sliders (weakening, erosion rate) sit inside the 16:9 frame; everything below the frame is presenter's notes. Hover any ⓘ or line for the rule behind it.
