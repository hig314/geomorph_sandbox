Limits to relief R(t)

Uplift and valley incision grow relief until landslides fail it. Seven steps, from a flat limit to earthquakes that both trigger landslides and damage the slope. The figure is a 16:9 slide: step through it with the arrow keys, full screen with F. On the last step two sliders (weakening, uplift rate) sit inside the frame; the circle at the lower left draws the same history as a ridge-and-valley slope; everything below the frame is presenter's notes. Hover any ⓘ or line for the rule behind it.
